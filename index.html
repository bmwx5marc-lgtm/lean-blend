THE FOUNDATION

Small serving.
Serious intent.

Every recipe in this edition is engineered around a 300 ml serving target. The final blended volume must be confirmed by a measured bench blend. The home cost reflects ingredient consumption only — no labour, packaging, overhead or profit.

ONE RULE

Measure the ingredients, blend until smooth, and use the nutrition and cost figures as practical guides. The recipes are designed for repeatable home preparation.

INGREDIENT INTELLIGENCE

Build with purpose.

LEAN BLEND uses food-first ingredients with useful nutritional roles. “Support” language is intentional: smoothies are not medicines.

GINGER

Distinctive flavour and a long history of use for digestive comfort. Start with a small amount.

CHIA + FLAX

Dense sources of fibre and useful fats. Increase portions gradually if your usual fibre intake is low.

BERRIES

Whole-fruit sources of fibre and polyphenols, with strong flavour at relatively modest portions.

KEFIR

Fermented dairy option for people who tolerate it; useful as a creamy smoothie base.

OATS

Whole-grain carbohydrate and fibre that can make a smoothie more substantial.

CITRUS

Excellent for brightness and vitamin C, but not appropriate for everyone who prefers a gentler or lower-acid option.

LEAN BLEND rule: more of an ingredient is not automatically better. Fibre, caffeine, citrus and strong spices should be adjusted to individual tolerance.
LEAN BLEND STANDARD

Useful nutrition.
No hype.

Protein and fibre can support fullness, while hydration supports fluid intake. LEAN BLEND does not present any smoothie as a magic fat burner, detox or hangover cure.
